Baldur’s Gate · Enhanced Edition Trilogy · Mod Install Guide

Piro’s Vanilla Supreme

A component-by-component install guide for one continuous Enhanced Edition Trilogy game — BG1, Siege of Dragonspear, BG2, and Throne of Bhaal — restored, repaired, and built around Sword Coast Stratagems.

Not for beginners. This build includes Sword Coast Stratagems, which assumes you already know how Baldur’s Gate combat works. If the trilogy is new to you, play it unmodded first. This guide is for intermediate-to-veteran players who can read a WeiDU prompt without fear.

Platform: Enhanced Editions on game patch 2.7 (built and verified on the GOG release, Windows). 523 components across 37 install steps in two folders. The pick lists and the checklist below come from one real, completed install, audited line by line.

What this build is

The goal is the original games, kept intact: cut content restored, bugs fixed, and combat made harder and smarter by SCS. It is not a content pack — no new-item or new-quest mods are included. Where a tweak makes the party stronger, SCS is the counterweight; where a bonus can apply to enemies as well, it does. Every job in the build has one owner — one shapeshifting rewrite, one banter pacer, one feud governor, one party AI — so the mods compose instead of colliding.

It plays as one game: BG1 runs into Siege of Dragonspear, then BG2 and Throne of Bhaal, with one interface, consistent companions, and SCS AI from the first kobold to the final boss.

Before you start

  • Work on copies. Copy both game folders somewhere safe and mod the copies. Keep pristine originals untouched — they are your backup and your uninstall.
  • Two folders, one order. Three mods install into the BG1EE folder first (DLC Merger → EE Fixpack → BG1 Unfinished Business). Then EET imports that folder, and everything else installs into the BG2EE folder, in the order given. Nothing ever installs “into EET” as a separate place.
  • WeiDU: use a current WeiDU executable (WeiDU releases). Windows Defender flags WeiDU and the EEex loader — both are known false positives; add exclusions for your two game folders and your mod staging folder.
  • GOG Siege of Dragonspear is a separate library product whose archive (sod-dlc.zip) goes to DLC Merger as-is. Do not unzip it manually.
  • Order is load-bearing. The sequence below is the compatibility work. In particular: EEex immediately after EET Core; LeUI before EEUITweaks; Ascension long before SCS; Infinity Sounds immediately before EET End; Enhanced Powergaming Scripts dead last.
  • Install means reading. Several mods ask sub-questions (variants). The pick lists name the exact variant taken wherever one exists.
  • Decide before you start. Uninstalling a single component later removes everything installed after it. Changing your mind mid-build means redoing the tail of the install, so work from the pick lists.

Install order & component picks

Steps 1–3 happen in the BG1EE folder. Step 4 returns to the BG2EE folder, and steps 4–37 all happen there. Expand any mod to see its exact component picks — these are the picks to reproduce this build, in plain names, in installer order where the log preserves it.

Things to look out for

This is not a beginner build

Sword Coast Stratagems — in any configuration — assumes you already know how Baldur’s Gate combat works. If this is your first time through the trilogy, play unmodded vanilla first; this guide will still be here, and you will get far more out of it.

Difficulty lives in the game, not the installer

After SCS installs, its slider maps to Basic (≈ vanilla Core Rules), Improved, Tactical, Hardcore, and Insane, changeable mid-playthrough. Most of this build’s SCS behavior scales with that slider — dragons gain triple hit points at Hardcore and above, for example.

Launch discipline

Always launch via InfinityLoader. Never let GOG Galaxy (or any launcher) verify or repair the modded copy — it will happily “fix” your mods away. Keep a pristine, unmodded copy of both games as your backup.

The main-menu rule for options

With this UI stack, in-game option changes are written only when you return to the main menu. Quit straight from gameplay and your flips are silently discarded.

Back up two files, not one

Keep BOTH folders’ WeiDU.log files (BG1EE side and BG2EE side) plus stratagems.ini from the BG2EE folder. SCS’s fine-tuning lives in the .ini and is invisible to the log; together, the three files are the build’s full fingerprint and its replay ticket (tools like Project Infinity can import a WeiDU.log; the .ini must travel with it).

The Slayer, concretely

Ascension’s Slayer grows at 2M / 4M / 6M XP with tiered reputation costs; this build reduces (does not remove) the reputation loss. Mastery scene: after the second Ellesime dream, sleep outdoors. If it never triggers, open the console and run: C:SetGlobal("MasteredTheSlayer","GLOBAL",1). Separately, one restored lich scene (Deril, in the druid grove quests) never triggers in this build — known, inert, and harmless; don’t chase it.

Never dismiss a companion in Brynnlaw

There is no return-home safety net in this build. A companion dismissed in Brynnlaw can be stranded there permanently. Dismiss people somewhere civilized.

Adalon has a clock

Take Adalon’s favor path and a two-day timer runs before the drow city turns hostile. The straight fight is fully supported — SCS’s improved drow make conquering Ust Natha afterward virtually impossible by design, but the normal quest path and the Adalon fight itself are unaffected.

Two IWD spells are imperfect in v11

Emotion: Hopelessness has an erratic projectile, and Emotion: Fear’s undead/construct exception may not fire. The build knowingly uses the stable v11 release anyway — just don’t build tactics on those two spells.

A few familiar fights are deliberately different

A small, select set of encounters was rebalanced on purpose (the Fair Fights picks), Ascension toughens every Throne of Bhaal boss, and Tougher Illasera in particular is a designed spike. If a fight feels “wrong,” check that list in your head before assuming a bug.

Money, bags, and smiths

Starting bags are bottomless, ammunition is weightless, stores sell higher stacks and buy everything, and both Cromwell and Cespenar forge at full speed with either smith able to make any item. Loot logistics are deliberately painless in this build.

Small print on people

Edwin, Jaheira, Minsc, and Viconia use their BG2 stats throughout (Anthology consistency picks), all five core companions use their BG2 appearance everywhere (EET Tweaks), romance race restrictions are removed, and stat gains your companions earn in BG1 do not carry into BG2 — stat books are best spent on your protagonist, whose stats carry natively.

Charms and scrolls behave a specific way here

Charmed creatures do not turn hostile when the charm ends. Scrolls cast at a fixed caster level rather than scaling with your level — treat scrolls as reliable mid-power tools, not late-game nukes.

One sword to keep an eye on

Dorn’s blade, Rancor, carries this build’s single deliberate exception to its no-extra-engine-hooks rule. It should behave perfectly normally — but if anything odd ever happens around Dorn’s sword specifically, suspect it first.

The auto-haste toggle can vanish after a reload

The +50% out-of-combat movement/casting speed (Tweaks Anthology) gives the protagonist a special ability to toggle it on and off. That ability is granted once, by a script that also records that it has been granted — and the record can outlive the ability: after a save/reload the button may be gone for good while the speed itself keeps working. To get the button back, open the console (Ctrl+Space) and run: C:SetGlobal("A7_AutoHasteAbility","GLOBAL",0) — the script then re-grants it within a few seconds.

Autosaves rotate

Interval saves cycle through four slots every 30 minutes. They are a safety net, not a save strategy — keep real saves at campaign seams (the SoD→BG2 transition especially, where banter cadence is worth a glance).

Reproducing or replaying this build

A saved WeiDU.log is machine-replayable when the mod files are staged: Project Infinity advertises a WeiDU.log import (community reports are mixed; its paste-a-sequence path is the reliable fallback), and the purpose-built mod_installer CLI installs directly from a log. Either way you need both folders’ logs, stratagems.ini, and the exact sources in the downloads table — master-branch mods replay whatever version is staged, so download them as close to together as you can.
